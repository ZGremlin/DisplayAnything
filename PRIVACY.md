# DisplayAnything Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 26, 2026**

DisplayAnything is a local-network app for viewing and controlling a computer from an iPhone or iPad. This policy describes the DisplayAnything iOS app.

## Information the app uses

DisplayAnything uses your local network to discover, pair with, and connect directly to computers running DisplayAnything. To make those connections work, the app processes device names, local network addresses, pairing information, and a device identity. The identity is stored in the iOS Keychain; app preferences are stored on your device.

When you connect to a computer, its screen is streamed directly to your device over an encrypted connection. Touch, pointer, and keyboard input you provide in the viewer is sent to that paired computer. This information is used to provide the features you request; the app developer does not receive it.

## How information is shared

DisplayAnything does not operate accounts, advertising, analytics, cloud storage, or relay servers. The app does not send your screen, input, pairing data, or device information to the developer. Connections are made directly between paired devices on your local network or a private VPN network.

The computer you choose to pair with receives the information needed for the connection and the input you send during a session. Only pair with devices you trust.

Apple may process diagnostic or crash information according to your device settings and Apple’s privacy policies. If you send feedback through TestFlight, Apple shares that feedback and associated diagnostic information with the developer.

## Permissions and controls

The app asks for Local Network access to find and connect to your computers. You can change this permission in iOS Settings. You can stop a session by disconnecting from the computer or closing the viewer.

## Data retention and deletion

DisplayAnything does not maintain developer-operated servers or receive app data to retain. Saved device information and the app’s device identity remain on your device until removed in the app or the app is deleted. You can remove a saved computer in the app and revoke Local Network access in iOS Settings.

## Children

DisplayAnything is a general-purpose utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app’s features or data practices change. The current version will be published at the privacy policy URL provided for DisplayAnything.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the [DisplayAnything GitHub repository](https://github.com/ZGremlin/DisplayAnything/issues). Please do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue.
